Journey

My class 10 result funnily enough made the local papers, which is cute and a little funny to me now.

By the end of my undergraduate degree, I had picked up a very diverse set of interests, ranging from neuroscience to theoretical physics and mathematics. I applied to a range of programmes reflecting that, including Applied Mathematics at Harvard, Theoretical Physics at Oxford, and Mathematics at the Max Planck Institute in Leipzig, and heard back positively. I chose Oxford because the scholarship there was independent of any specific advisor or project, which gave me the flexibility to tailor my own path once I got there, and I eventually moved from Theoretical Physics into Mathematics.

I currently sit on the Youth Advisory Board of the International Association for Premenstrual Disorders (IAPMD), a nonprofit supporting people affected by PMDD and related conditions. I'm passionate about destigmatizing PMDD and related disorders, and about helping young people navigate education systems, find meaningful support, and redefine success in ways that honour their health, needs, and lived realities. I also feel strongly about championing women in STEM, and about building systems without cracks for people to fall through.
