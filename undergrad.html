Undergrad+

Before mathematical physics, my undergraduate and Master's years at IIT Kanpur (B.Tech + M.Tech, Materials Science and Engineering, with minors in Quantum Mechanics, Statistical Physics, and Cognitive Science) were spent on a wide range of projects. Here are some of the ones I enjoyed most — click through to read the actual reports, surveys, and slides.

Materials Science

Spiral Eutectic Microstructures in Zn–Mg Alloys

Synthesized a Zn–Mg alloy at eutectic composition and imaged the self-organising spiral eutectic microstructure with optical and scanning electron microscopy, then used EDX to work out why and how the spirals formed.

Report

Crystallographic Texture and Fatigue in Titanium

Used electron backscatter diffraction and fractography to study how crystallographic texture from cold working affects dwell-fatigue behaviour in commercially pure titanium.

Presentation (PPTX)

Transparent Flexible Electrodes via the Crackle Technique

Fabricated a micron-thin metallic mesh network using the crackle technique, and proposed an Ag–Adenine 1D-polymer nanowire matrix as an electrode or hole-transport layer for large-area flexible electronics.

Poster

A Refractory High-Entropy Alloy for Spacecraft Applications

Characterised the phase structure and high-temperature mechanical properties of a novel MoNbTaWV refractory alloy made by vacuum hot pressing of ball-milled powders — a candidate for replacing ceramics in high-stress, high-temperature spacecraft components.

A Survey of Chip Manufacturing for Wearable Healthcare Devices

A term paper surveying fabrication techniques for smart wearable healthcare devices, written for a course on materials processing.

Survey

Biomaterials: Bacterial Adhesion on Implant Surfaces

A survey of how the adhesion force of Staphylococcus aureus varies across different biomaterial surfaces, written for a course on biomaterials.

Physics

Reproducing the Hofstadter Butterfly in Twisted Bilayer Graphene

Used the Bistritzer–Macdonald continuum model to find the zero modes of twisted bilayer graphene, and reproduced the Hofstadter butterfly pattern in its Landau-level spectrum under a magnetic field.

Report Slides

Correlation Energy of the 2D Electron Gas

Computed correlation energy for many-electron systems using Density Functional Theory, comparing analytic and numerical techniques against semi-analytic wavefunctions for the two-dimensional electron gas.

Report

A talk given to Jürgen Jost's research group at the Max Planck Institute for Mathematics in the Sciences, Leipzig, on why the partition function of a 2D minimally supersymmetric QFT is an integral modular function.
